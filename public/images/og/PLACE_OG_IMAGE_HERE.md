# OG Image

Save your Open Graph image here as:

  `public/images/og/og-image.jpg`

Specifications:
- Dimensions: 1200 × 630 px (required for all platforms)
- Format: JPG
- Content: Use any of the boardroom/portrait photos as a base, add your name + tagline as text overlay

This image appears when your site link is shared on WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Facebook, iMessage, etc.
